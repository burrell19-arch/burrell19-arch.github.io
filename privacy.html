Privacy Policy

How the Naruto YT Playlist Organizer handles Google and YouTube data.

Access and purpose

The organizer works with Lillith Archives. Its read-only grant lets it read channel identity, playlist titles and IDs, visibility, playlist contents and ordering, and video IDs and availability. Its separate writer grant is used for authorized playlist creation and adding or ordering approved videos. The organizer uses these data to verify the channel, compare the roster, prevent duplicates, and verify and resume approved work.

Storage and service providers

Google handles sign-in and authorization. The organizer does not receive the Google password. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are kept in the operator’s private Windows profile or, for hosted runs, in GitHub Actions secrets for a private repository. Temporary access tokens allow the organizer to make authorized requests. Playlist IDs, video IDs, progress, and error and quota information may be stored in private local files, the private repository’s state branch, and private GitHub Actions reports. Hosted reports are configured to expire after 14 days. Local files and private checkpoints remain while the operator needs them for operation or recovery.

Google and GitHub process information needed to provide the API, hosting, and workflow services. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or model training. This public site contains no playlist records or credentials and sets no app cookies or analytics. GitHub Pages may retain server logs for security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Revoking access and deleting stored data

The authorized user can revoke a grant in Google Account connections. To request deletion, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen. The operator removes applicable stored OAuth credentials and Google user data from local files, private checkpoints and history, and hosted reports, as appropriate, within 30 days of revocation or a deletion request. Revocation and deletion are handled by the operator. Playlists already saved on YouTube are managed separately through YouTube.

The current policy is posted here. The operator will inform the authorized user before a material change in how the organizer uses Google user data.
